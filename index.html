GenAI · Recruitment AI

Hirely — AI Recruitment & Candidate Matching

An AI-powered recruitment backend for candidate–job matching, resume parsing, and candidate ranking, served by a tested FastAPI service layer.

  • Resume parsing with Gemini and Docling, plus 768-dimension Gemini embeddings for semantic matching.
  • Deterministic skill matching combined with semantic similarity in a 50/20/30 hybrid scoring engine, e.g. a 95% overall match with 100% skill and 84% semantic sub-scores.
  • Modular backend on PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, and Alembic migrations, with Pydantic validation.
  • JWT authentication and role-based access control for Candidate and Recruiter roles.
